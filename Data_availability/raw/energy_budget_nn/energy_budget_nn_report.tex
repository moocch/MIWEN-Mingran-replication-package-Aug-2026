\section*{Convention (paper, Sec.~II\,D / Methods~G)}
$e_{\mathrm{ip}} = e_1+e_2+e_3$ with
$e_1 = P_x T_{\mathrm{air}}/(4N\,\eta_{\mathrm{radio}})$ (transmit; $P_x$
\emph{at the mixer}, $\eta_{\mathrm{radio}}=10\%$),
$e_2 = 6\,e_{\mathrm{ADC}}/4N$, $e_3 = 8\,e_{\mathrm{dig}}/4N$
($e_{\mathrm{ADC}}=e_{\mathrm{dig}}=1$\,pJ; fixed 14\,pJ read-out fee per
answer). Component-level and input-referred on both sides: no DAC, no static
transceiver power, no SDR wall-plug; H100 reference 70\,fJ per real MAC
(arithmetic only). One complex inner product of length $N$ = $4N$ real MACs.

\section*{2.\ Comb CNN at $(P_{LO},P_{RF})=(-3,-65)\dbm$ at the mixer}
The data comb is inserted at the mixer RF port at $-65\dbm$ --- the
operating point of the frozen-battery demonstration as physically
delivered. Client pays the data comb only (weights = base-station side). Symbol counts from the fielded planner
(\texttt{plan\_layer}, as in \texttt{miwen\_overhead\_ratio.py});
r3plus: 7.21\,M complex MACs = 28.85\,M real MACs,
31,659 answers, 1,628 symbols ($+3.1\%$ sync) $\to$
\textbf{2.84\,s airtime/image}.

The NN budget is \emph{read-out--fee dominated}, the opposite of Sec.~D's
large-$N$ points: the CNN's inner products are short
($D=75\ldots1600$), so the fixed 14\,pJ/answer amortizes poorly (conv1 pays
46.7\,fJ per real MAC by itself). The comb packing is not the
problem --- airtime works out to 98\,ns per real MAC,
essentially Sec.~D's 117\,ns.

\section*{3.\ Serial CNN at $(P_{LO},P_{RF})=(-10,-24)\dbm$}
The measured low-drive serial arm
(\texttt{serial\_nn\_runner\_m10m24.py}, unpadded chain $\to$ commanded
$\approx$ device plane). One complex product per 3.2\,$\mu$s slot:
7,211,904 slots $\to$ \textbf{23.1\,s airtime/image}. Read-out fee
assumes the slow coherent integrate-and-dump read-out at the \emph{row}
rate (Sec.~D convention); the as-fielded per-sample SDR capture would
instead cost 16.0\,pJ per real MAC.

\section*{Bottom line}
\fcolorbox{black}{yellow!15}{\parbox{0.94\textwidth}{
\textbf{Sec.~D reproduces exactly} (1.47 / 0.59\,fJ per real MAC, crossover
$N\!\approx\!50$, bend $N\!\approx\!6.5{\times}10^3$).
\textbf{Comb CNN at $(-3,-65)\dbm$: 452\,nJ/image =
15.7\,fJ per real MAC, 4.5$\times$ below the H100 line} ---
but unlike the large-$N$ inner products the budget is 98\% read-out fee,
because the network's inner products are short; the radio itself is nearly
free (0.31\,fJ). \textbf{Serial CNN at $(-10,-24)\dbm$:
919\,$\mu$J/image = 31.9\,pJ per real MAC,
455$\times$ above H100} --- serial's \emph{radio} term is
$\sim$10$^5\times$ the comb's ($+41$\,dB power, $-24$ vs.\ $-65\dbm$,
$\times$ 8$\times$ airtime per MAC, 0.8\,$\mu$s vs.\ 98\,ns);
the \emph{totals} differ 2{,}000$\times$ because both pay the same read-out
fee, which dominates the comb budget. At the headline
$(0,0)\dbm$ serial point $e_1$ grows another $+24$\,dB ($\approx$8\,nJ per
real MAC). Frequency multiplexing, not the mixer, is what makes the NN
energy story work.}}
