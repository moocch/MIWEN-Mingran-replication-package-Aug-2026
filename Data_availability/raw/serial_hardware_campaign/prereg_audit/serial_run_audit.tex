\section{Claim under test}
In time-serial encoding each product traverses the mixer at its own
amplitude, so the device transfer curve acts \emph{inside} the
summation and cannot be corrected at readout. The experiment compares
two networks of the identical certified architecture, executed on the
identical hardware protocol: one trained assuming an ideal multiplier
(``clean''), one trained \emph{through} a measurement-calibrated
Shockley-like digital twin (``twin''). $2\times2$ = \{clean, twin\}
$\times$ \{digital, hardware\}. \textbf{No noise injection in either
arm; no bias correction anywhere.} Operating point $(0,0)$\dbm{}
commanded, both ports; chain: the 10\,dB IF pads as installed.

\section{Encoding and readout (inference protocol)}
One product per 32-sample slot (3.2\,\textmu s): non-negative
activations as RF slot envelopes on subcarrier $+2.2$\,MHz; weights as
LO slot envelopes on $-1.8$\,MHz, \textbf{sign and phase carried on
the slot carrier} (complex weights encoded directly --- both
quadratures used, as in the certified $|Y|$ era). Every product lands
on the single difference tone; 8-sample guards at slot edges. Chirp
preamble for timing only. Readout: the slots of one output row are
summed \emph{coherently} (the digital emulation of an analog
integrate-and-dump at the IF), then the \textbf{magnitude} is taken
--- so the absolute capture phase cancels and \emph{no phase
reference is used or needed}. Between layers: the certified $|Y|$
interpass unchanged (folded BatchNorm, ReLU, maxpool, per-image
max-norm); decision $\arg\max S^2$. Calibration per arm: one frozen
drive scale per layer (the layer's slot-stream RMS, fixed from a
10-image batch so every chunk runs at one operating point) and one
scalar gain per layer (least squares of $|$row sum$|$ vs ideal).
Chunks are row-aligned (no row ever spans two captures). No debias:
the certified chain's Rician correction is unnecessary at row-sum
SNR, and its absence is deliberate and gated.

\section{The twin (training physics)}
Mingran's Shockley-derived core with the documented corrections ---
two distinct one-pole knees (May: LO $+2.17\pm0.19$, RF
$+3.66\pm0.20$\dbm), the reciprocity-sign fix, effective (not
physical) thermal voltage --- plus $K{=}20$ tanh ridges for the
map-vs-equation residual (May: 0.89\,dB held-out; adding ridges
beyond $K{\approx}30$ buys nothing against the 0.65\,dB repeat
floor). Coefficients are \textbf{fitted to our port-calibrated USRP
CW map}, with the datasheet and the May parameters as external
plausibility gates --- fitted values, not datasheet values, because
family-typical specs carry 2--3\,dB tolerance against a 0.9\,dB
model budget. Lift to a signed slot transfer by exact ring odd
symmetry: $f(x,w)=\operatorname{sgn}(xw)\,A(|x|,|w|)$. Excluded, per
plan: thermal noise and memory. Leakage is handled exactly as in the
code: the \emph{fit's} measurement model for CW-map cells augments the
product term with two per-port leakage powers ($10^{(l_1+P_{LO})/10}$
and $10^{(l_2+P_{RF})/10}$; fitted: LO-side absent at $-210$, RF-side
$\approx-102$\,dB) --- instrument terms that de-bias the map reading
and are \textbf{excluded from the fielded twin}, whose slot decode
rejects that content by construction. Gates as finally frozen: G2 held-out $\le 1.3$\,dB on
the product-dominated fit region, both ports $\ge -35$\dbm{}
(achieved: \textbf{0.12\,dB}); G3 the assembled twin must
\emph{predict} the independently measured serial slot curve at
$(0,0)$, duty-corrected, fitted on none of that data (achieved:
\textbf{3.7\% worst bin}). G1 --- named knee values vs the May fit
--- was \textbf{waived and demoted to reported-only}: the measured
surface's compression is joint (sum-type, cf.\ the May analysis's own
sum-oriented ridges), so separable per-port knees are not
identifiable on this map; an honest physical finding, documented
rather than forced. In training the surface is evaluated from a
$256^2$ bilinear table (max deviation 0.069\,dB from the analytic
form, verified). The twin enters
\textbf{training only}: the forward applies $f$ to every product
before summation; at inference the real mixer applies the real $f$.
Nothing twin-related exists in the inference path of either arm.

\section{The equations, before and after the corrections}

\subsection*{Instantaneous device law (draft-era form; retired)}
The published manuscript's device law (its Eq.~2) is the symmetric
$\sinh\!\times\!\sinh$ ring transfer, which contains no feedthrough
half-term and needs no correction; the fielded sign convention
$\operatorname{sgn}(xw)$ \emph{is} that law's odd symmetry. The form
below, and the corrections applied to it, belong to a pre-arXiv draft
and are retained here as provenance only ($w$ = LO port, $x$ = RF
port, $V_T = k_BT/e \approx 25.85$\,mV):
\begin{equation}
y(t) \;=\; \frac{w(t)}{2} \;+\; \frac{V_T}{2}\,
\ln\!\left[\frac{e^{x(t)/V_T} + e^{-w(t)/V_T}}
               {e^{x(t)/V_T} + e^{w(t)/V_T}}\right].
\end{equation}
Corrections established by the May analysis and the August
refinement: (i) the \emph{effective} thermal voltage must be used,
$V_T \to V_T^{\rm eff} \approx 165$\,mV ($\approx 6\times$ the
physical value; port-to-diode voltage division $\sim 1/6$ through
transformers and matching); (ii) the explicit feedthrough half-term
carries the wrong port --- the formula's predicted LO$\leftrightarrow$RF
asymmetry \emph{anti-}correlates with the measured surface
($r=-0.66$); swapping the port roles in that term,
\begin{equation}
y(t) \;=\; \frac{x(t)}{2} \;+\; \frac{V_T^{\rm eff}}{2}\,
\ln\!\left[\frac{e^{w(t)/V_T^{\rm eff}} + e^{-x(t)/V_T^{\rm eff}}}
               {e^{w(t)/V_T^{\rm eff}} + e^{x(t)/V_T^{\rm eff}}}\right],
\end{equation}
flips the asymmetry correlation to $r=+0.64$.

\subsection*{Power-surface hybrid (May form, AWG dataset)}
\begin{equation}
P_{\rm IF}(P_{\rm LO},P_{\rm RF}) = \mathrm{w2dBm}\!\left[
\alpha\, S(P_{\rm LO};P_{\rm LO,sat})\, S(P_{\rm RF};P_{\rm RF,sat})
+ P_{\rm leak}\right] + \sum_{i=1}^{10} w_i
\tanh(a_iP_{\rm LO} + b_iP_{\rm RF} + c_i),
\end{equation}
$S(x;x_{\rm sat}) = x/(1+x/x_{\rm sat})$ (one-pole, linear power);
fitted knees $P_{\rm LO,sat}=+2.17\pm0.19$, $P_{\rm RF,sat}=
+3.66\pm0.20$\,dBm, $\alpha = 27.62\pm0.18$\,dB, $P_{\rm leak} =
-73.87\pm0.07$\,dBm --- all matching the ZEM-4300+ datasheet within
2--3\,dB.

\subsection*{The frozen serial twin (final form, USRP map)}
Product term only, fitted on the product-dominated region (both
ports $\ge -35$\dbm; the feedthrough-dominated edges are excluded
because the slot decode rejects carrier-frequency content), $K=20$:
\begin{equation}
P^{\rm dB}_{\rm prod}(P_{\rm LO},P_{\rm RF}) = a_0 +
10\log_{10}\!\big[\tilde S(P_{\rm LO};k_{\rm LO})\,
\tilde S(P_{\rm RF};k_{\rm RF})\big]
+ \sum_{i=1}^{20} w_i \tanh\!\Big(\frac{a_iP_{\rm LO}}{40} +
\frac{b_iP_{\rm RF}}{40} + c_i\Big),
\end{equation}
with $\tilde S(P;k) = u/(1+u)$, $u = 10^{(P-k)/10}$. The knee values
are \emph{reported, not gated}: the measured compression is joint
(sum-type; per-port knees unidentifiable on this surface). Held-out
0.12\,dB on the region; serial-slot cross-validation 3.7\% worst bin.
Lift to the signed slot transfer by exact ring odd symmetry, with the
frozen drive mapping (unit-RMS slot streams at commanded $(0,0)$):
\begin{equation}
f(x,w) = \operatorname{sgn}(xw)\cdot
10^{P^{\rm dB}_{\rm prod}(p_{\rm lo}(|w|),\,p_{\rm rf}(|x|))/20},
\qquad p(\,a\,) = 20\log_{10}\!\frac{a}{\mathrm{rms}(\text{stream})}.
\end{equation}
In training, $f$ is applied to every product before summation
(evaluated from a $256^2$ bilinear table, max deviation 0.069\,dB);
at inference the mixer applies the real $f$; nothing twin-related
exists in either arm's inference path.

\section{Session discipline and gates (all pre-registered)}

frame; gain vs the frozen big-frame reference ($0.1155\pm20\%$;
measured session-to-session repeat 0.6\%) and residual shape in
$[0.30, 0.75]$ --- the decode-corruption detector. Chain gate:
first-capture payload peak within band of the 2026-08-21 diagnostic.
Sync margin $\ge 50$ (typ.\ $\sim$1750). Calibration saved and
reloaded across the split halves (H1 = battery images 0--149, H2 =
150--299) so both halves run identical frozen corrections. No
accuracy gates on either arm: both outcomes reportable. Pre-pinned
digital predictions (computed before the corresponding hardware
data is examined): clean digital 99.50 (verified); clean-under-twin,
twin-under-twin, twin-under-ideal --- pinned once the twin passes
G1--G3.

current status and all intermediate failures are logged in the

\section{Reading of the outcome}
The two twin-validation checks both passed: (i) clean-hardware
(6.33) landed at the pinned clean-under-twin forecast's
``collapse'' verdict --- the twin \emph{forecast} an untrained
network's fate; (ii) twin-hardware (97.67) landed 2 images from
twin-under-twin (98.33) --- twin-in-the-loop training \emph{is} the
serial regime's hardware-awareness mechanism. Together with the comb
program (where the same device curve in training produced 2--10\%
pilots and linear calibration sufficed at 98.83), the pair of
experiments demonstrates that \emph{the encoding, not the device,
decides which abstraction training needs}: nonlinearity outside the
sum is a readout detail; nonlinearity inside the sum requires a
physical twin in the training loop.
